# Asset provenance

- `land-110m.json`: Natural Earth geographic data, packaged by world-atlas 2.0.2. Retrieved 11 September 2026 from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/world-atlas@2/land-110m.json . Natural Earth data: public domain. Source project: https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas .
- `globe.svg`: generated locally by `scripts/build.py` from that geometry. Connection paths illustrate coverage and are not measured transport routes.
- The four platform screenshots in `images/` and `favicon.png` were present in the original repository. Confirm rights and freshness when replacing or republishing screenshots.
- Editorial card illustrations and the social card are original code-native graphics. They are not documentary evidence.
- `energy-horizon.png`: created with the built-in image-generation tool for this website. It depicts a fictional industrial terminal, is labeled as a visual concept, and is not documentary evidence. The final prompt and production notes are in `docs/HERO-ART-DIRECTION.md`.
- The wordmark and inline crossing-line symbol are derived from the existing EchoFrame brand mark.
- DM Sans and Instrument Serif are loaded from Google Fonts. Both font families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. System fonts are used if the external font request fails.
