How coverage is built

Around your question

Exposure usually sits far from the capital, in places where reporting is thin and nobody is watching between one commissioned report and the next. We find the local newsrooms that already know the ground, license their archive, and set up collection for the places that matter to you.

We work across Latin America, Africa and South Asia, for clients in energy, life sciences and finance. Tell us where you operate and we will tell you plainly whether we cover it, and what it would take if we do not yet.

Longest-running programme

Venezuela

Venezuela is the working programme. Two years of archive, a structured event record for the eastern oil states, and a correspondent agreement in progress.

Collection sits in the oil states of the east and the west, outside the capital, where the exposure is largest and the reporting is thinnest. Scope, delivery and access are agreed for each engagement.

The same method on a different record

European energy and regulatory policy

It looks like a different business and it is not. In Venezuela the problem is that almost nothing is written down. In Brussels the problem is that everything is, continuously, in volumes no team can read. Both are the same failure, which is a public record that exists but cannot be used as a record. One is scarcity and one is volume.

The tracker is available to clients on request.

Defence and security

Decision support for planners

For defence and security institutions we are developing decision support for planning around energy and critical infrastructure in contested environments. A person stays in command of every decision, and each prediction and suggested option can be traced back to the sources behind it.

We are developing it with TALISMAN, a Paris crisis-communications and strategic-advisory firm active in defence and security. TALISMAN will co-design the operational scenario the system is tested on, and will run the table-top exercise that shows whether planners find its output useful and trustworthy.

This work is in development. We will say so here until it is in use.
