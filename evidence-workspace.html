When accounts differ,
follow the evidence

Each source needs to be read for what it establishes. Compare the activity, location and observation period before deciding whether accounts agree or conflict. Keep the original source and the reason for your assessment together.

What travels with the evidence

01

Provenance

Source reference, publisher, underlying reporting origin, publication time, collection time, and permitted use.

02

Scope

Event time, place precision, named actors, activity described, and the part of the claim actually observed.

03

Review history

Grouping rationale, independence check, contradictions, analyst interpretation, and the next review trigger.
