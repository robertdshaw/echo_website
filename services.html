Products

What you get,
and what is included

Continuous data from places that are hard to reach, volatile and largely offline, delivered as an alert, a brief, a detailed report, or answers to your questions. Three subscriptions run against the assets and questions you name. Projects are single pieces of work with a scope and an end.

Subscriptions

Coverage that keeps running

A subscription is coverage, not a login. Access to the record comes with any of them and is not sold on its own. You are told the day something is confirmed rather than at the end of a reporting cycle.

01 / Subscription

Asset Watch

Continuous monitoring of the assets you name, with an alert the day a development is confirmed.

What is included
  • An alert on the day a development is confirmed, with the sources and what it affects.
  • A weekly brief for each asset after an analyst has read it.
  • Every claim in the brief carries a reference back to the record, so anything can be traced.

Where Eastern and western Venezuela today. Colombia, Mexico, Rwanda and Pakistan as collection is built.

02 / Subscription

Question Book

Written questions with a date and a rule for settling them, each scored when the date arrives.

What is included
  • The question set, agreed with you and then frozen so it cannot drift.
  • A probability on each with the developments that moved it, inspectable down to the sources.
  • An answer and a score on the date, kept as a record over time.
03 / Subscription

Ground Truth

Reporting filed from the place itself, joined to your asset coverage rather than published as articles.

What is included
  • A record within hours of filing, with what happened, where, who was involved and how the reporter knows it.
  • It reaches you before the national press has the story, and often when the national press never runs it at all.

Where Only where the correspondent network exists. We will tell you plainly whether that covers where you are exposed.

Projects

Single pieces of work

Each has a defined scope and an agreed deliverable. A scoping conversation comes first and carries no charge.

  • Viability assessment

    A legal and contractual read of one asset against six criteria, each citing the document it rests on. Refreshed for twelve months whenever a development touches that asset.

  • Question design

    Turning a decision you have to make into a set of questions that can actually be settled, with the evidence rules written down. Most questions people bring us cannot be answered as asked. This is the work of fixing that.

  • Zone brief

    A single deep read of one state or one cluster of assets, built from the record and ending with the questions worth running and what would settle them.

  • Scoping conversation

    Defining the subject, the decision and the horizon before any mandate. No charge.

What each project covers

What it costs

Pricing depends on the number of assets, the number of questions and whether ground reporting is included. We will tell you what your coverage would cost in the first conversation, and what it would not cover.
