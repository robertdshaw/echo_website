## Central assessment

Write the current judgment in no more than 50 words. If the evidence is insufficient, say what remains unresolved.

## Evidence

Link the source register. Identify which claims each source establishes, whether sources are independent, and any contradictions.

## Competing explanation

State the strongest alternative and what would distinguish it from the current assessment.

## Reader implications

Explain how the evidence relates to the original research question. Avoid transaction recommendations or legal determinations beyond the agreed scope.

## Visual and supporting material

Specify the question the visual answers, underlying data, period, units, source attribution, accessibility text, and rights to publish.
