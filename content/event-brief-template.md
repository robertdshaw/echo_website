## What remains disputed

List incompatible claims, possible differences in scope, and alternative explanations.

## Effect on the question

Explain whether a review is warranted. A numerical update requires a documented, operating inference process; otherwise leave it unassigned.

## Viability review, if relevant

Identify affected criteria, source documents, previous assessment dates, and the specialist review needed. Missing documentation means unassessed.

## Next verification step

Name the observation that could distinguish the competing accounts and when to revisit the issue. Do not include confidential source identities or reporting routes.

## Public-release review

Confirm permitted source use, non-identifying narrative, safe geographic detail, attachment handling, and clearance of any quotations. Use a fictional example if public disclosure is not appropriate.
