# Venezuela programme: public content and production

Adapted from the supplied working document dated 9 September 2026. This is a website/content translation of that plan, not a fresh audit of the Venezuela repository or an implementation of its collection, GIS, corroboration, or forecasting services.

## The proposition

EchoFrame develops intelligence around the asset, contract, actor, and deadline that a client decision depends on. Venezuela leads the development story. The intended differentiator is an inspectable event record that retains official positions, local accounts, physical observations, and unresolved disagreements.

The website now presents:

- A Venezuela programme page with proposed collection zones and a fictional evidence brief.
- A homepage led by asset-level questions and a revised essay, “Attention is not evidence.”
- An eight-stage public methodology that identifies development status.
- Guides on conflicting accounts, question resolution, and zone-level collection.
- An inquiry form that asks for client perspective, subject, deadline, and decision context.

The other regional platforms remain in the coverage section. This plan does not establish their implementation status, so no new claims about those platforms have been inferred.

## Three content products to produce first

### 1. The question card

One named subject, one outcome, one horizon, and a written resolution rule. Capture the client archetype: operator, entrant, creditor, or supplier. Include a rule for missing or contradictory resolution evidence. The public sample uses a fictional terminal and assigns no probability.

Use `content/question-register-template.csv`. Agree an initial set of roughly 10–15 questions only after client needs and available evidence are understood; that number is a proposed starting scope, not a public service entitlement.

### 2. The event and disagreement brief

Show the client question, event identity, time references, permitted location precision, and the claims that support or dispute it. Explain how each source knows. Keep source independence separate from source count. End with the next verification step, reviewer, and review trigger.

Use `content/event-brief-template.md`. Public examples must be fictional or explicitly cleared for disclosure. Do not move restricted reports, reporter identifiers, raw attachments, precise sensitive locations, or identifying narrative detail into this repository.

### 3. The asset viability note

Pair operational evidence with a separate documentary assessment of contract standing, licence and sanctions standing, arbitration exposure, counterparty solvency, operational access, and security of tenure. Each criterion has a document reference and assessment date. Missing support means unassessed.

A relevant event can request a review; it cannot, by itself, settle the contractual or legal interpretation. Publish only rights-reviewed excerpts and conclusions within the analyst's agreed scope.

## Two refinements made when translating the plan

**Source independence.** Different owners and home cities help screen for relationships, but they do not prove that two accounts are independent. Both may repeat the same original report. Public copy therefore requires tracing the evidentiary chain.

**Forecast evaluation.** A Brier score is an error measure, not proof of skill in isolation. Specify the forecast timestamps, treatment of unresolved questions, weighting across questions, and reference forecast before reporting results. The plan alternates between averaging updates and scoring the final probability; that choice needs to be settled before implementation. See [ECMWF's verification guide](https://www.ecmwf.int/sites/default/files/elibrary/2007/15489-verification-probability-forecasts.pdf).

Also keep evidence state and freshness distinct: an event may be contradicted and stale at the same time. Hand-set source or event weights are assumptions to review and evaluate; the site does not represent them as statistically validated likelihood ratios.

## Source rights and protection

The legal analysis in the working plan is not reproduced as a public assurance. A specific rights basis, retention rule, and permitted output need appropriate review for each source and use. The website neither certifies text-and-data-mining compliance nor claims a TrustFrame attestation.

Source protection is described as a design requirement. It is not represented as a backend control implemented by this website. In particular, the new contact form is an ordinary email-draft tool, not a secure correspondent intake channel. It tells users not to include confidential or source-identifying details.

## Working with prospective contributors

Privately prepare a sample zone brief and an evidence-needs document before discussing supply or distribution. Ask advisers for client decisions and resolution questions. Ask subject specialists for documented assumptions and source material they can license for the intended use. Negotiate reporting independence and safety responsibility before any field commissioning.
